01 — About

About me

My name is Darren Fang and I'm a recent Computer Science: Game Design graduate from UC Santa Cruz.

Throughout my time as a game design student, I worked on a variety of projects and took on diverse roles, including lead programmer, level designer, team lead, and art lead. These experiences allowed me to develop a broad range of skills across game development.
